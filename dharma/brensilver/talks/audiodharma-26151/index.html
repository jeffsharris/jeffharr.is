An assignment - crafting a lovingkindness practice for yourself

AudioDharma 03:55 2026-08-22

What if lovingkindness practice had to be tailored so closely to your own heart that it felt almost uncomfortably specific? The talk frames this as a short workshop: devise, write, and practice a kindness meditation that is native to you, not polished for anyone else. It emphasizes that the right words may be simple, quiet, and even slightly challenging before they become medicine.

  1. 00:30 The assignment - The talk introduces the task of creating and practicing a kindness meditation that is devised for oneself.

  3. 02:17 Simple, not ornate - The speaker suggests that the most helpful phrases may be plain, quiet, and not necessarily the most elaborate expressions.
  4. 02:40 Workshop the words - The next stretch is framed as a period to experiment with and refine a highly personalized lovingkindness practice.
  5. 03:21 Native to the heart - The talk closes by returning to the idea that heart practice is often about finding words that feel natural and fitting.
