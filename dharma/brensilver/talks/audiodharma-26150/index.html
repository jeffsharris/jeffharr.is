The four species of love as you move around the world

AudioDharma 05:20 2026-08-22

The talk asks how loving-kindness can move beyond a single feeling and become a responsive practice in ordinary life. It sketches the four Brahma-viharas as four species of love—loving-kindness, compassion, sympathetic joy, and equanimity—and shows how they balance and support one another. From there, the focus turns to walking practice: noticing what kind of care a person, a moment, or a scene seems to call for, and offering a simple, precise wish in response.

Chapters

  1. 00:00 Opening the practice - The talk begins with the day’s movement into instructions, a short period of walking, and then dialogue and questions.
  2. 00:54 Four species of love - The four Brahma-viharas are introduced as distinct forms of love that balance and refine one another.
  3. 01:54 What each one meets - Each quality is briefly defined by the kind of experience it meets: suffering, good fortune, and the limits of control.
  4. 02:34 Reading the moment - The walking period is framed as an opportunity to sense what kind of care a person, tree, or situation may need.
  5. 03:26 Small data, careful response - The speaker describes noticing the shape of another person's heart-mind with limited information and offering a fitting response.
  6. 04:09 Simple wishes for others - Examples of concise phrases show how kindness can be tailored to what is seen: ease, soothing, connection.
  7. 04:55 Closing and questions - The talk returns to the bell, quiet sitting, and a final invitation for questions about practice.
