Guided Meditation: Stillness and movement

AudioDharma 55:12 2026-11-20

The talk turns on a simple but unsettling question: what if stillness does not care about our self-story, our preferences, or even the person we imagine we are becoming? Through repeated phrases and gentle insistence, the speaker invites listeners to rest in a stillness that undercuts struggle and softens the ways we fight ourselves. The final movement is less about attaining a special state than about learning how to meet silence with care and trust.

Chapters

  1. 41:09 Entering Stillness - The meditation opens by naming stillness as something impersonal and beyond ordinary self-concern.
  2. 41:51 Beyond Pleasant and Unpleasant - The teaching widens into a reminder that stillness is not organized around comfort, discomfort, or moral self-evaluation.
  3. 43:04 Not Becoming Something - The speaker repeatedly returns to the idea that stillness is not interested in the identity project of becoming.
  4. 44:03 Stillness Beckons - The refrain deepens into an invitation to stop, letting the listener feel how stillness gently calls rather than demands.
  5. 46:19 Settling Into Repetition - The repeated phrases become more spacious, allowing the emphasis on release and non-striving to land through rhythm.
  6. 48:19 What We Are Becoming - The language shifts slightly toward what is actually unfolding, while still refusing the pressure to manage spiritual progress.
  7. 50:06 Misunderstanding Stillness - The talk names inner conflict as a misunderstanding about stillness itself, reframing self-struggle as something to see differently.
  8. 52:54 Closing Blessing - The meditation closes with gratitude, a light touch of humor, and a final encouragement to take good care of silence.
