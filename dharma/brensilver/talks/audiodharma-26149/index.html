Matthew Brensilver Dharma Talks

Guided reflections on kindness

AudioDharma 32:01 2026-08-22

What if kindness is not something we manufacture, but something already here that we learn to notice? The talk moves from a spacious “yes” to simple practices of breathing, softening shame, and inwardly wishing well for oneself, treating kindness as a patient way of seeing rather than a forced feeling. It ends by framing the Brahma-vihara as a place to dwell, where time can help love ripen and hatred become less tenable.

Chapters

  1. 02:00 Settling into yes - The talk opens by entering silence and introducing the idea of a patient, unwavering yes without pretending everything is pleasant.
  2. 04:30 Breath and activation - Breath is offered as a way to enliven what is numbed and soothe what is overly activated.
  3. 08:39 Love versus clinging - The speaker contrasts clinging, with its complexity and strategizing, with the simplicity of love and small signs of goodness.
  4. 15:22 Seen with kindness - The reflection turns toward being seen through infinite kindness and meeting whatever appears without forcing a conclusion.
  5. 18:31 Wishing well inwardly - The talk moves into simple phrases of metta and the practice of listening for their effect in body and mind.
  6. 24:22 Beyond shame - Shame is contrasted with remorse, and the listener is encouraged to meet what is not yet love with respect and patience.
  7. 28:39 A place to dwell - The closing section names the Brahma-vihara as a dwelling-place and presents kindness as patient, steady, and quietly transformative.
