Guided Meditation: Breathing with your life

AudioDharma 25:16 2026-09-02

How closely can you listen to silence, and what happens when the breath becomes the place where life is fully felt? This guided meditation turns attention toward breathing as a way of meeting thinking, bodily sensation, love, longing, and loss without being cut off from them. The practice moves from simple listening and settling into a more inclusive sense of breath, where awareness can stay very simple while still honoring the whole of life.

Chapters

  1. 00:00 Listening for silence - The meditation opens by inviting very close listening, even toward the silence that may be present beneath noise.
  2. 01:53 Breath as fresh sensation - Attention shifts to the breathing as both familiar and new, with an emphasis on letting breath sensations fully fill awareness.
  3. 02:53 Settling into practice - The guided settling deepens as the speaker continues to orient the mind toward simple, steady contact with the breath.
  4. 05:34 Why thought persists - Thinking is framed as a useful form of coping and protection, which helps explain why it is hard to let go of.
  5. 07:58 Undoing defensiveness - When the moment feels safe enough, the invitation is to become undefended and place hope into this breath.
  6. 15:30 Breath and the whole of life - The teaching widens the frame, showing breath as a place where body, feeling, memory, longing, and loss are all honored.
  7. 16:46 Keeping it simple - The meditation continues in a very simple, quiet way, holding experience without adding complexity.
  8. 21:21 Awareness includes more - The closing points to a basic rejoicing in being here and to awareness as something larger than whatever is immediately present.
