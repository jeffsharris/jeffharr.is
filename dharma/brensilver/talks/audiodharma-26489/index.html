Guided Meditation: Letting the voltage course through

AudioDharma 26:36 2026-10-21

What if practice is less about getting rid of experience and more about widening the system that can hold it? The talk uses the image of “voltage” to explore how awareness can be stabilized so that love, sensation, suffering, and memory can move through without strain, grasping, or resistance. It returns again and again to the question of whether the mind can trust experience enough not to clamp down when intensity arises.

Chapters

  1. 00:12 Beginning in Love - The practice opens by centering the mind on one point of love, if that can be done without strain.
  2. 00:53 Softening the Moment - The talk settles into breathing, softness, and the sense that this is the atmosphere for the whole practice.
  3. 02:03 Training for Voltage - Dharma practice is framed as training to expand what the system can hold without coping or delusion.
  4. 03:23 Stabilizing Awareness - The emphasis shifts to holding phenomena fully so awareness can stay steady rather than fragmenting under pressure.
  5. 05:36 When Experience Bumps Up - Thought, feeling, memory, and sound are examined as they meet fear and trigger grasping, hating, or other forms of coping.
  6. 09:21 No Resistance - The talk asks whether suffering can be met exquisitely, with enough openness that it does not clink into anything in the mind.
  7. 16:33 Deeper Fixations - The practice moves beneath the surface to deeper holding, energetic blockages, and the possibility of tolerating them more fully.
  8. 20:16 Background and Doing - The closing stretch is partly obscured, but the thread suggests a reflection on habitual doing and how it continues before anything happens.
