Guided Meditation: Anicca and Feeling

AudioDharma 41:35 2026-07-29

What happens if feeling is not a problem to solve but the very place where the mind, craving, and self-making can be known? The talk moves from compassion and forgiveness into a close investigation of vedanā: how arousal and valence shape moods, stories about self, and the push to control experience. It then settles into a guided practice of relaxing, staying, and letting awareness saturate feeling so change can be directly seen.

Chapters

  1. 00:51 Begin with forgiveness - The practice opens with compassion for oneself and a broad forgiveness toward body, mind, world, and phenomena.
  2. 04:45 Feeling as the center - The talk frames feeling as the place where phenomena converge and where moods quietly confirm our experience.
  3. 07:39 Arousal and valence - Emotion is described as beginning with bodily feeling, with attention to arousal, pleasantness, unpleasantness, and neutrality.
  4. 11:53 Thought charged with feeling - Self-stories and inner drama are shown as thoughts energized by feeling, and the invitation is to release friction around them.
  5. 14:31 Consent and relax - The guidance emphasizes consenting to feeling, softening the guarding of the self, and repeatedly relaxing.
  6. 20:15 Craving and promise - The talk turns to craving, noticing how objects overpromise and how the imagined gain is made of feeling and marked by impermanence and nonself.
  7. 23:39 Known directly - The listener is encouraged to see feeling in discomfort, urge, memory, and even judgments about the meditation, and to let awareness saturate it.
  8. 28:59 Noticing change - The practice settles into calmly noticing change without taking a fixed vantage point on life.
  9. 36:46 Rebirth of the next moment - The final movement explores how we keep predicting ourselves into the next moment and ends by returning to the basic rhythm of feeling and arousal.
