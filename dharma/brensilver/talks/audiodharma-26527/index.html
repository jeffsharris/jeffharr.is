Matthew Brensilver Dharma Talks

Lightly Guided Meditation

AudioDharma 26:49 2026-10-28

This talk explores how practice can move from effort and self-conscious control toward reverence, gentleness, and ease. It asks what it means to sit within a wider lineage of care, and how attention becomes nonviolent and impeccable without strain. The closing emphasis is simple: notice how grasping and resistance make experience feel solid, then soften that congealing tendency with kind attention.

Chapters

  1. 00:00 Settling and arriving - The talk opens with a brief welcome and a shift from joking around to simply sitting down together.
  2. 01:21 Sangha and lineage - Attention is drawn to community, ethical restraint, and a sense of belonging to a long lineage of wisdom and love.
  3. 04:38 Reverence in attention - The practice turns toward what can be bowed to right now, inviting reverential attention and nonviolence.
  4. 09:28 Effort and grace - The talk explores how diligent effort in practice can condition effortlessness, while keeping clear about what is and is not our job.
  5. 11:42 Working with the mind - The instruction continues through repeated reminders to stay with what is present, using effort without grasping at results.
  6. 23:13 Softening what solidifies - Near the end, the talk names how grasping and resistance turn experience into fixed things, and points back to gentle attention.
