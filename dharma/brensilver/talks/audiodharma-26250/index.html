Guided Meditation: Developing Learning Readiness

AudioDharma 25:40 2026-09-09

What if no moment in meditation is wrong, and the work is simply to stop adding struggle? The talk moves from that basic trust into a set of instructions for letting go: putting down what can be put down, listening without preference, and noticing how tension shows up as greed, hatred, or lingering. It closes by reframing practice itself as learning readiness rather than acquisition, a way of entering experience with a heart-mind prepared to know suffering, peace, and conditioning more clearly.

Chapters

  1. 00:12 Every Moment Good Enough - The talk opens by framing the Dharma as a field where no moment is wrong and each moment can point toward silence and release.
  2. 02:13 Put Down What You Can - The instruction turns practical: stop everything else, not by suppressing thought, but by setting aside whatever can be released.
  3. 05:03 Listening Without Preference - When thoughts obscure silence, they are heard as part of practice, with emphasis on relinquishing preference about what arises.
  4. 08:54 Tension and Relaxing - The talk names lingering, greed, and hatred as forms of tension and asks how deeply relaxation can be practiced.
  5. 10:05 Stopping the Fight - The practice challenge becomes ceasing to fight with ourselves, which depends on trusting that each moment is good enough.
  6. 13:15 Wisdom and Compassion - This trust is connected to the development of wisdom and compassion, showing why acceptance matters beyond comfort.
  7. 19:34 Learning Readiness - The closing teaching reframes meditation as developing readiness to learn from experience rather than trying to force insight.
